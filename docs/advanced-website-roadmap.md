# Advanced Website Roadmap

## Goal

Turn ZDT Realty from a broad feature collection into a sharper product with:

- stronger buyer trust
- better lead capture
- paid seller value
- cleaner premium UX

## Phase 1: Conversion Foundation

- Gate contact reveal on cards and property details pages.
- Track lead-grade events such as phone unlocks, calls, chat opens, and visit requests.
- Standardize trust messaging across listing cards, details pages, and marketplace pages.
- Replace low-value generic CTAs with intent-rich actions such as:
  - `Show Number`
  - `Schedule Visit`
  - `Request Brochure`
  - `Message Seller`

## Phase 2: Premium Seller Product

- Make owner plans visibly tied to workflow value:
  - ranking priority
  - lead management access
  - analytics depth
  - promotion credits
  - verified eligibility
- Turn listings and dashboard pages into upgrade-aware work surfaces.
- Expose plan ROI in owner workspace instead of keeping pricing isolated.

## Phase 3: Discovery Upgrade

- Add faster search and filter combinations on marketplace pages.
- Surface category-level trust and market signals, not only listing grids.
- Package promoted inventory as clear sponsored experiences instead of subtle section-level boosts.
- Improve homepage narrative so buyers and sellers both understand why the platform is better.

## Phase 4: Advanced Product Layer

- Property details:
  - deeper trust signals
  - locality and demand context
  - richer visit and enquiry flow
- Owner workspace:
  - operating dashboard
  - lead pipeline health
  - next-best-action recommendations
- Marketplace:
  - smart filter presets
  - category intelligence
  - monetization surfaces for builders and owners

## Build Order

1. Lead capture consistency
2. Seller plan packaging
3. Marketplace filtering and trust layer
4. Homepage premium positioning
5. Reporting and attribution improvements

## Product KPIs

- contact unlock rate
- call click rate
- message-start rate
- schedule-visit rate
- lead-to-deal conversion
- seller upgrade rate
- boosted listing usage
- repeat buyer sessions

## Immediate Next Pass

- Extend lead-gated contact access to all major detail views.
- Add `Request Brochure` and `Price Sheet` flows.
- Add sponsored card variants for promoted properties.
- Tie owner analytics and seller upgrades to the new lead events.
