# ZDT Realty Founding Round Pitch Deck Notes

## 1. One-line positioning

ZDT Realty is a full-stack real estate platform that combines property discovery, decision intelligence, builder/developer tools, owner monetization tools, and admin-grade compliance workflows in one product.

## 2. Short founder pitch

ZDT Realty is not just a listing website. It is a real estate operating platform built around trust, transparency, and execution. A buyer can discover, compare, message, and evaluate properties with market and infrastructure context. An owner can list, track leads, monitor analytics, and monetize visibility. Builders and dealers get a company portal to manage projects, teams, leads, and promotions. On top of that, ZDT adds high-trust modules like government e-auctions, infrastructure tracking, market intelligence, group deals, and apartment management.

## 3. Core problem we solve

- Buyers struggle with fragmented information: listings are separate from market data, infra updates, builder trust, and decision tools.
- Sellers and owners struggle to manage listings, lead flow, subscriptions, and performance from one place.
- Builders and dealers need more than a profile page. They need project operations, team access, marketing tools, and compliance structure.
- Admins need strong moderation, approval workflows, role controls, auditability, and platform-wide visibility.
- Real estate decisions in India often depend on trust, documentation, local infrastructure, and verified source discovery, not only photos and price.

## 4. Product vision

Build a trusted real estate intelligence and transaction-enablement layer for India, where discovery, due diligence, communication, growth tools, and operational control are connected inside one platform.

## 10. 90-second interview pitch script

ZDT Realty is a real estate platform built to solve a deeper problem than just listing properties. In the current market, buyers have to move between listing sites, infrastructure news, market research, builder trust checks, and direct calls. Builders and owners also lack a clean operating layer for managing projects, teams, leads, and visibility. What we are building is a full-stack platform that connects all of this.

On the consumer side, users can buy, rent, compare, save, message, and evaluate properties with market insights and infrastructure context. On the business side, owners get dashboards, analytics, subscriptions, and lead tracking. Builders and dealers get a company portal to manage projects, promotions, team access, and compliance-ready listings. On the trust side, we have differentiated modules like government and bank e-auctions, group buying, infrastructure trackers, and circular building material reuse.

So the vision is not to become another property listing site. The vision is to build a trusted real estate intelligence and operations platform for India.

## 11. Interview talking points if they ask “what makes it different?”

- We are multi-sided: buyer, renter, owner, builder, team operator, admin.
- We combine discovery and operations, not only listings.
- We use trust and verification as a product layer.
- We have India-specific workflows like infrastructure tracking, UPI support flow, and official e-auction sourcing.
- Our monetization is diversified across subscriptions, promotion tools, and later enterprise integrations.

## 12. What to say carefully

- Present AI design modules as roadmap/innovation, not mature core modules.
- Present owner payments page as a billing layer under development if asked for payment maturity.
- Present early supporters as a community-belief signal, not investment capital.
- Present group deals and e-auctions with compliance clarity:
  - ZDT does not collect group booking money
  - ZDT does not conduct auctions
