# Advanced Product Roadmap

## Goal

Turn ZDT Realty from a broad portal into a focused premium real-estate product with:

- stronger buyer trust
- cleaner lead capture
- better seller monetization
- clearer premium workspace value
- more disciplined discovery and conversion flows

## Product Pillars

### 1. Buyer Funnel

- search by city, project, locality, and trust state
- property pages that help users decide faster
- lead-protected contact unlocks
- premium CTAs like brochure, price sheet, loan support, and site visit

### 2. Seller Workspace

- listings, leads, analytics, boosts, and subscriptions must feel like one operating system
- every seller page should explain what improves conversion and what is plan-gated
- premium plans should unlock visibility, control, and reporting, not just more rows

### 3. Monetization

- sponsored listing slots
- priority ranking and boosts
- lead analytics and CRM access
- verified trust packaging
- paid seller subscription tiers

### 4. Trust Layer

- verified badges and moderation
- fraud reporting
- gated phone access
- cleaner listing quality signals
- stronger marketplace trust filters

## Phase 1: Foundation

- gate phone reveal on cards and details pages
- track unlock and call actions as lead events
- make details pages stronger with buyer decision support
- make subscriptions clearer with outcome-based packaging
- make owner dashboard explain growth levers better

## Phase 2: Premium Discovery

- add better filter intelligence
- add locality insights and micro-market content
- improve featured and sponsored listing surfaces
- improve homepage sections that connect buyer demand to seller upgrades
- add ranking logic tied to seller plans

## Phase 3: Seller Revenue Engine

- real sponsored card product
- premium placement inventory and controls
- boost management with reporting
- seller lead funnel reporting
- verified listing commercial packaging

## Phase 4: Billing and Operations

- payment checkout and plan activation
- invoice and billing history
- admin approval and promotion workflows
- analytics exports and attribution views

## Phase 5: Advanced Marketplace

- map-first search
- saved search intelligence
- project discovery and launch intelligence
- finance and loan partner funnels
- locality landing pages for SEO

## Immediate Build Order

1. Finish buyer decision support on property details
2. Extend owner dashboard and subscription ROI messaging
3. Improve marketplace trust and premium filters
4. Wire plan entitlements into ranking and boosts
5. Complete checkout and billing
6. Add deeper locality and map intelligence

## Success Metrics

- more phone unlocks per active listing
- more schedule-visit requests per verified listing
- better lead-to-listing conversion
- higher seller upgrade rate from free to paid plans
- higher share of verified listings
- better response rate on featured and boosted listings
