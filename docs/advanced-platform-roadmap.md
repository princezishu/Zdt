# Advanced Platform Roadmap

## Objective

Turn the current portal from a broad feature collection into a focused real-estate product with:

- stronger buyer discovery
- measurable lead capture
- clear seller monetization
- consistent premium UX

## Track 1: Product Direction

1. Build around a single core loop:
   - discover listing
   - qualify interest
   - capture verified lead
   - route lead to owner or builder workspace
   - monetize visibility and workflow access
2. Treat buyer tools and seller tools as one connected system, not separate pages.
3. Remove mixed messaging around "free", "beta", and "pre-revenue" as monetization becomes active.

## Track 2: Buyer Experience

1. Upgrade property details into a decision page:
   - gated contact reveal
   - buyer decision metrics
   - verified visit workflow
   - stronger trust messaging
2. Upgrade listing discovery:
   - quick trust filters
   - ready-to-move filters
   - featured and promoted visibility
   - category intelligence blocks
3. Keep compare, saved searches, and area insights visible as a decision stack.

## Track 3: Seller Revenue Product

1. Make subscriptions control real entitlements:
   - listing quota
   - CRM access
   - analytics access
   - boost credits
   - verified eligibility
2. Productize promoted visibility:
   - featured inventory
   - top search placement
   - sponsored cards
   - homepage placement
3. Give owners a premium control layer:
   - growth actions
   - quota status
   - lead pipeline status
   - monetization ladder

## Track 4: Data and Analytics

1. Treat `unlock_phone`, `call_click`, `schedule_visit`, and `message` as revenue-grade signals.
2. Surface those signals inside owner analytics and subscription upsells.
3. Reduce reliance on local demo inventory across public discovery flows.

## Delivery Order

1. Lead gating and buyer decision support on property details
2. Marketplace quick filters and premium seller upgrade surfaces
3. Homepage advanced decision stack and seller workspace promotion
4. Owner dashboard and subscription commercialization
5. Owner analytics integration for the new lead events

## Success Metrics

- higher message-to-visit conversion
- more verified contact unlocks
- more boosted or upgraded listings
- higher owner plan upgrades
- lower friction from search to decision
