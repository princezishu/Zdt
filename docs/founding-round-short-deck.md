# ZDT Realty Short Founding-Round Deck

## Slide 1. Title

**ZDT Realty**  
Trusted Real Estate Intelligence + Operations Platform

Subtitle:
One platform for buyers, owners, builders, and real estate workflows.

## Slide 2. Problem

- Property discovery is fragmented across listings, market data, infra updates, and trust checks.
- Buyers struggle to make confident decisions with incomplete information.
- Owners and builders need lead, analytics, and workflow tools, not just listing pages.
- Real estate platforms often lack admin governance, verification, and operational control.

## Slide 3. Solution

ZDT Realty combines:

- Property marketplace
- Decision intelligence
- Trust and verification workflows
- Builder and dealer operating tools
- Owner monetization tools
- Admin control systems

## Slide 4. Consumer Product

- Buy and Rent marketplaces
- Property details with enquiry, callback, and site-visit requests
- Favorites, compare, saved searches, notifications
- Real-time messaging
- AI assistant and WhatsApp entry points

## Slide 5. Intelligence Layer

- Real estate news feed
- Market price tracker
- City-to-city price compare
- Upcoming project announcements
- Area intelligence
- Government infrastructure tracker with verification filters

## Slide 6. Trust and Differentiation

- Government and bank e-auction source directory
- Group purchase deals with verified builder filter
- Compliance-first workflows
- Circular Build for reusable construction materials
- Platform focus on verified, transparent real estate flows

## Slide 7. B2B Growth Engine

- Builder/dealer company portal
- Project creation and approval workflows
- Team access control
- Lead management and promotion tools
- RERA-ready listing structure
- Subscription and enterprise expansion path

## Slide 8. Owner Growth Engine

- Owner dashboard for listings, leads, views, and conversion
- Analytics and subscription upgrades
- Boosted visibility and premium plans
- Profile, KYC, and payout profile setup

## Slide 9. Admin and Scalability

- Listing moderation and fake inventory control
- Team and role management
- Promotions, support-program controls, and audit visibility
- Source management across infra, insights, and e-auctions
- Real-time backend workflows and role-based platform governance

## Slide 10. Business Model + Closing

Revenue model:

- Owner subscriptions
- Builder subscriptions
- Featured listings and promotional placements
- Sponsored projects and banner inventory
- Premium analytics and later enterprise integrations

Closing line:

ZDT Realty is evolving from a listing portal into a trusted real estate operating system for India.
