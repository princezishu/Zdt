## Design guidance

- Keep each slide to 3 to 5 bullets only.
- Use screenshots from your strongest modules, not every page.
- Prioritize these visuals:
  - Homepage
  - Buy or Rent detail page
  - Insights or Infrastructure
  - Builder portal
  - Owner dashboard
  - Admin desk
- Mark clearly what is live and what is roadmap if asked.
